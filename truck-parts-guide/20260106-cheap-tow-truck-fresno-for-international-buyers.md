**Q: how to operate rollback tow truck**

Operating a rollback tow truck typically involves a few key steps. First, inspect the truck, hydraulics, winch, straps, and safety equipment before use. Then position the truck on stable ground and set the parking brake. Use the hydraulic controls to tilt and lower the bed. Attach the winch cable securely to the vehicle, ensuring proper anchoring points, and slowly winch the vehicle onto the bed. Once loaded, center the vehicle, secure it with approved straps or chains, and return the bed to travel position. Always check lights, brakes, and tie-downs before moving. For safe operation, trained and certified personnel are strongly recommended.
