## FAQ

**Q: what is the best duster for dusting**

Three checks save most buyers from expensive mistakes on a special truck: (1) real chassis age and mileage — demand the VIN and factory certificate; (2) body build quality — inspect welds, hydraulics and pump brands; (3) after-sales — confirm the supplier ships a spare-parts kit with the truck and answers warranty claims in writing. Expect to pay $28,000-85,000 FOB for a properly specced unit.

**Q: what is a truck water pump**

A special truck is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. Most units ship by RO-RO or flat-rack container with lead times of 30-60 days.

**Q: how long are tow trucks**

A tow truck operates on a standard truck chassis with a power take-off (PTO) driving the specialized equipment: hydraulics raise, lower or drive the working body while the operator controls the cycle from the cab or a panel. Daily checks cover PTO engagement, hydraulic oil level and body mounting bolts. Export units usually run a 338 hp diesel engine matched to the body's demand, keeping fuel burn predictable at roughly 18-25 L/100 km depending on load.

**Q: how to get dump truck loads**

Mechanically, a dump truck is a chassis plus engineered body: the engine drives both the wheels and, through the PTO, the working system. Control is electro-hydraulic, with safety interlocks that stop the body when the truck moves. Chinese export models are designed for simple maintenance — most service points are reachable with standard tools, and factories ship a spare kit (filters, seals, hoses) with each unit.
