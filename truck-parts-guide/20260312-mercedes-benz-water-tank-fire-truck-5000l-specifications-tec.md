**Q: what is a semi truck cab card**

A semi truck cab card is a document that lists the vehicle’s registered specifications and operating authority details. It typically includes information such as the truck’s VIN, license plate, registered weight, axle configuration, carrier name, and sometimes the states or jurisdictions where the truck is authorized to operate.
