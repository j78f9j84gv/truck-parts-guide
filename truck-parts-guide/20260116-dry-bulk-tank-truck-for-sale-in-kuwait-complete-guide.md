**Q: how to wire a semi trailer plug**

Wiring a semi trailer plug depends on the plug type, trailer lighting system, and the tractor’s pin configuration, but the process is generally straightforward for a qualified technician. First, confirm whether you are using a 7-way or 7-pin connector and follow the standard wiring diagram for your region. Typically, the wires are connected to ground, tail lights, left and right turn/brake lights, auxiliary power, and ABS or reverse circuits. Use proper gauge wire, weatherproof connectors, and secure grounding to avoid intermittent faults. If you need the correct harness or replacement plug, prices usually range from about USD 15 to USD 80, depending on quality and configuration.
