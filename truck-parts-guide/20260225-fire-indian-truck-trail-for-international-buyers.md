## FAQ

**Q: how does a vacuum truck work**

A special truck operates on a standard truck chassis with a power take-off (PTO) driving the specialized equipment: hydraulics raise, lower or drive the working body while the operator controls the cycle from the cab or a panel. Daily checks cover PTO engagement, hydraulic oil level and body mounting bolts. Export units usually run a 260 hp diesel engine matched to the body's demand, keeping fuel burn predictable at roughly 18-25 L/100 km depending on load.

**Q: what is a truckload carrier**

A special truck is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. Most units ship by RO-RO or flat-rack container with lead times of 30-60 days.

**Q: what fire brigade**

“Fire brigade” usually refers to a fire truck or fire engine, which is a specialized emergency vehicle used by fire departments to respond to fires, rescues, and other incidents. Depending on the configuration, it may include water tank capacity, a pump system, hoses, ladders, rescue tools, and emergency lighting. In the market, prices vary widely by specification, brand, and whether the vehicle is new or used. A realistic range is typically USD 80,000 to 300,000+ for a standard unit, while advanced or custom-built fire trucks can cost significantly more.
