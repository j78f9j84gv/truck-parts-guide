**Q: how to draw a truck step by step**

To draw a truck step by step, start with basic shapes. First, sketch a long rectangle for the truck body and a smaller square or rectangle for the cab. Next, add two or more circles for the wheels, making sure they are aligned evenly. Then draw the windows, doors, mirrors, and grille to define the front section. Add details like the bumper, headlights, fuel tank, and wheel arches. After that, refine the outline, remove unnecessary guide lines, and strengthen the final contours. If you want a realistic look, include shading and simple perspective. For special trucks, such as dump trucks or tankers, adjust the cargo section accordingly.
