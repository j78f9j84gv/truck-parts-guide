## Field Notes

One of the biggest mistakes is matching the truck only to capacity, not to actual operating conditions. A buyer may select a larger tanker for better economics, only to find that local bridges, depot entrances, or loading bays cannot handle the axle load or turning radius. Another common error is underestimating product compatibility. A truck used for diesel in one contract may later be expected to handle gasoline, and that changes sealing, valves, and safety requirements.

The most widely used configuration is the tanker truck, especially for diesel and gasoline distribution. A typical HOWO 8X4 400HP fuel tank truck is often chosen by buyers who need high payload and strong climbing ability for long inland routes. It commonly carries around 20,000 to 30,000 liters, with a price range of roughly USD 55,000 to 85,000 depending on tank material, pumping system, and export options. For tighter roads and mixed urban-rural use, a howo 6x4 fuel tank truck is a practical middle-ground choice, often priced around USD 48,000 to 72,000.
