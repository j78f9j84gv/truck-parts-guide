## FAQ

**Q: what's tipper truck**

A tipper truck, also known as a dump truck, is a special-purpose vehicle designed to transport loose materials such as sand, gravel, soil, coal, or construction waste. It is equipped with a hydraulic lifting system that raises the cargo bed so the load can be discharged quickly and efficiently from the rear or side. Tipper trucks are widely used in construction, mining, road maintenance, and agriculture. Depending on payload capacity, axle configuration, and brand, a new tipper truck typically costs around USD 40,000 to USD 180,000+, while used units may be lower.
