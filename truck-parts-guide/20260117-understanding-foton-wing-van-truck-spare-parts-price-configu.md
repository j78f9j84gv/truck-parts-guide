For buyers importing a FOTON wing van truck spare parts price from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

Compare fuel or electricity consumption, maintenance intervals, tire size, refrigeration service points, and expected resale value. Overseas buyers who import from China usually succeed when they match the truck to real routes, not just to a catalog photo.

**Q: do rubber tree plants make rubber**

Rubber tree plants themselves do not directly “make” rubber in the final usable form, but they do produce the natural latex sap that is the raw material for natural rubber. This latex is collected from the tree, then processed and refined into rubber products used in tires, seals, hoses, gloves, and many industrial applications. If you are asking about special trucks for latex collection or transport, the vehicles are typically configured to handle liquid collection tanks, safe transfer, and clean transport conditions. **Q: how large is a box truck**

A box truck, also called a cube truck or straight truck, typically ranges from about 10 to 26 feet in box length, depending on the application. Smaller box trucks are often around 10–12 feet and used for local deliveries, while medium and larger models can be 14–26 feet for moving, logistics, and freight transport. Overall vehicle size usually depends on the chassis and load capacity, with gross vehicle weight ratings commonly from about 10,000 to 26,000 lbs.
